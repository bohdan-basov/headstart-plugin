# Signals

A signal type is a public event, a freshness window and where it is read. Six ship because a
host can read them today. The brief may add a type by naming its event and its source.

| Type | Event | Default window | Read where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hiring | Live roles on the company's own board; a count threshold scaled to headcount | 14 to 60 days | ATS boards, job post pages, X |
| Funding | A round announced, with what the money is for | 30 to 90 days | Press, press releases, X |
| About to raise | Proxies: stealth emergence, first roles posted, a batch or accelerator listing, a launch, the founder's own building posts | 60 days | X, batch directories, Product Hunt |
| Warm path | Shares an investor, batch, employer or community with the customer | none | Portfolio and batch pages, field 6 |
| Stated ask | A founder asks for or complains about the service | 30 days | X |
| Launch | A product launch | 30 days | Product Hunt, X |

Warm path is the strongest type and the customer's own list, read whole at Sweep. Stated ask is
the weakest: alone it produced two of ten worth a message.
