# Name

Find the decision maker field 2's role rule names, from public pages, and confirm two things
about them: the role, and a public URL the customer can reach them at.

## Where the name is

- The job post's "reports to" or hiring-team line: it names the leader the role answers to.
- The company's team or about page.
- The LinkedIn guest profile, `linkedin.com/in/<slug>`, when a name is in hand.
- The X profile: `read_x_profile` confirms a role and a public URL when the person is on X;
  one read per named person, after the name is known.
- Press and podcast pages, which name a CEO or a CTO with the company.

## Who holds the decision

Headcount moves the hiring owner: the founder to about 15 people, the first recruiter at 15 to
30, a Head of People from 30 to 150, a talent director above about 300. At a company under 15
the founder is the leader of every role. The recruiter named on a job is a route to the leader,
never the lead; a posted title without a name ("reports to our Head of CX") is a lead still
unnamed.

Read a person to confirm role and URL and stop there: the opener context comes from what the
company is doing, in Why, and a buyer's timeline adds nothing to it.

## Parked

A company that fits with no person named within three reads goes to field 8 as
`fits, person not found`, with its domain, signal URL and `stopped at Name`. It is kept, retried
at Name in the next run, and counted in the report's gap line.
