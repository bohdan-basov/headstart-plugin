# Why

Write each lead as the block in `SKILL.md`. Every line is a fact with its URL, or the word
`inferred` where a read did not land.

## Why now

The why-now comes from what the job text or the press release says the company is trying to do.
The round and the job post themselves are what every other vendor congratulates; their content
is what nobody else leads with: the BDR role that moves the company "from inbound to outbound",
the round that is "to open a US go-to-market", the vault platform the engineers will own.

## Opener context

One fact the customer leads with, in the company's own words where they exist, quoted and
linked. The customer writes the message; the block hands them the fact.

## Evidence

A URL per claim: the signal, each size read, the stage, the person's role, the warm path. A
claim with no page behind it says `inferred` and names what it was inferred from.

## Example

    1. Akshay Mehta, Founder at Demoverse (demoverse.ai)
       Person: https://x.com/Akshay_MehtaAM
       Company: no team page, no careers page, one maker on Product Hunt, size inferred under
       five; pre-seed.
       Signal: funding, Sep 22, https://x.com/Akshay_MehtaAM/status/2102272733686210696
       Fit: in; rules 1–6 checked, none fired. Software with published tiers at $499 and $999 a
       month (https://demoverse.ai); engineering hires none, inferred from the Product Hunt
       maker list (https://www.producthunt.com/p/demoverse/introducing-demoverse) and the
       absence of a team page.
       Why now: the $600K pre-seed led by Lumikai is "to turn early trials with Indian brands
       into paid campaigns and to open a US go-to-market"
       (https://startuptalky.com/news/demoverse-raises-600k-pre-seed-lumikai/).
       Opener context: his own line, "Spent the last few weeks testing this with brands and
       consumers. Now we get to execute even faster", and the paid campaigns and US rollout the
       money is for.
       Warm path: none.
       Evidence: URLs above; team size inferred.
